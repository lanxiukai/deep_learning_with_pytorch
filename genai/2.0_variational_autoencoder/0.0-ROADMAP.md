# Variational Autoencoder Learning Roadmap

This directory implements the eight VAE variant units retained by the
companion reading guide. The scripts are algorithm lessons, not a production
training framework: each entry keeps its direct baseline, objective,
probability interface, gradient boundaries, checkpoint identity, and sampling
path visible. Shared network blocks and evaluation utilities live in
`dl_utils/vae/`.

The file groups encode the recommended learning order. They do not imply that
beta-VAE, cVAE, IWAE, or later branches accumulate all earlier changes.

## 1. Completed Baseline

| Lesson | Role |
|---|---|
| `0.1_autoencoder.py` | Deterministic reconstruction baseline |
| `1.0_vae.py` | Standard diagonal-Gaussian VAE and reparameterization |
| `1.1_vae_evaluation.py` | Posterior reconstruction versus prior generation |

These completed introductory scripts retain their original 256x256 face
setup. Most later variant units use compact 32x32 models. The beta-VAE lesson
deliberately reuses this introductory setup so its training objective is the
only algorithmic change from the standard VAE.

## 2. Step 1: Beta-VAE

| Lesson | Direct baseline | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `2.0_beta_vae.py` | `1.0_vae.py` | Scale the complete per-sample KL by beta while sharing data, model, optimizer, and training loop | A directly inspectable `D + beta * KL` objective |
| `2.1_beta_vae_evaluation.py` | Matched standard-VAE and beta-VAE checkpoints | Compare both models on the same images and prior samples | Rate-distortion, per-dimension KL, active units, reconstruction, and collapse diagnostics |

These capacity diagnostics do not establish semantic disentanglement because
the face dataset has no independent ground-truth generative factors.

## 3. Step 2: Conditional VAE

| Lesson | Direct baseline | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `3.0_conditional_vae.py` | Standard VAE | Condition the posterior and decoder; enable a conditional prior only as a controlled branch | Conditional distortion/rate and per-class prior samples |
| `3.1_conditional_vae_evaluation.py` | cVAE training entry | Separate training-time and generation-time information paths | Condition compliance, within-condition diversity, prior ablation, and shuffled-condition checks |

The target image is available to the recognition posterior during training
but never to the prior used for generation.

## 4. Step 3: Importance-Weighted Autoencoder

| Lesson | Direct baseline | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `4.0_iwae.py` | Standard VAE | Replace the one-sample ELBO with a stable per-example K-particle bound | K=1 identity, ESS/K, gradient statistics, and matched compute budgets |
| `4.1_iwae_evaluation.py` | IWAE runs with different K | Re-evaluate every run with the same large-K estimator | Bound uncertainty, rate, active units, reconstruction, and prior samples |

K changes the objective estimator and compute budget, not the posterior or
decoder family.

## 5. Steps 4-5: Hierarchical and Ladder VAEs

| Step and lesson | Direct baseline | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 · `5.0_hierarchical_vae.py` | Standard VAE | Add a second latent, `p(z1 | z2)`, two KL terms, and top-down sampling | Per-layer rates, active units, generation order, and layer closing |
| 5 · `5.1_ladder_vae.py` | Two-level HVAE | Fuse bottom-up evidence with the matching top-down prior by precision | Matched generator/capacity comparison and per-layer utilization |
| `5.2_hierarchical_vae_evaluation.py` | Both retained hierarchy models | Intervene on one stochastic layer at a time | Posterior/prior replacement and layer-closing counterfactuals |

The two scripts share the same generative hierarchy so the Ladder comparison
isolates its inference-network increment.

## 6. Steps 6-7: VQ-VAE and FSQ

| Step and lesson | Direct baseline | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 · `6.0_vq_vae.py` | Autoencoder | Learned vector quantization, STE, codebook loss, and commitment loss | Reconstruction, active codes, entropy, frozen PixelCNN prior, and generation |
| 7 · `6.1_fsq.py` | Matched VQ-VAE interface | Replace learned lookup and commitment loss with fixed scalar levels | Matched reconstruction, effective rate, token utilization, prior NLL, and generation |
| `6.2_discrete_tokenizer_evaluation.py` | Both retained tokenizers | Compare tokenizer and downstream-prior behavior under one protocol | Distortion, effective rate, reconstruction distribution, and prior generation |

Tokenizer training and prior training are separate stages. A SHA-256 model
state identity binds every prior to the exact frozen tokenizer that produced
its tokens.

## 7. Step 8: VQGAN

| Lesson | Direct baseline | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `7.0_vqgan.py` | VQ-VAE plus completed GAN training skills | Train with frozen learned LPIPS, delayed PatchGAN, controlled adversarial weighting, and a frozen-token Transformer prior | Two-optimizer isolation, bounded training validation, tokenizer/prior identity, and auditable run metadata |
| `7.1_vqgan_evaluation.py` | Completed VQGAN stage artifacts | Independently reload the tokenizer, discriminator, and prior; compare named VQGAN runs and an optional VQ-VAE system baseline | Paired fidelity, reconstruction distribution, token rate, prior NLL, PatchGAN diagnostics, and complete generation |

Real training uses learned LPIPS v0.1 with a frozen VGG trunk. The explicitly
named random-feature loss exists only inside the offline smoke test and is not
a substitute metric. The fixed adversarial-weight baseline should be
established before enabling the gradient-ratio strategy. Use `--run-name` to
keep fixed, adaptive, and loss-ablation artifacts in separate directories;
the evaluation entry applies the same held-out protocol to every selected run.
It is an evaluation companion, not a ninth retained model.

## 8. Resource, Checkpoint, and Evaluation Contract

- Every named model has an independent training entry, configuration,
  checkpoint identity, and direct-baseline record.
- Shared Gaussian, hierarchical-latent, quantizer, token-prior, and PatchGAN
  components do not remove a model's own training, generation, and evaluation
  responsibilities.
- Gaussian lessons save final weights only when another script consumes them;
  these short lessons do not implement resume machinery.
- Discrete tokenizers save stage artifacts because the tokenizer must be
  frozen before prior training. Reconstruction, prior generation, and
  downstream latent generation remain distinct paths.
- Smoke tests use synthetic tensors, execute the model-specific backward
  paths, and do not download datasets or pretrained weights.
- The route ends after VQGAN and hands off to pixel-space DDPM. KL
  autoencoders and Latent Diffusion remain in `../3.0_diffusion_model/`.
