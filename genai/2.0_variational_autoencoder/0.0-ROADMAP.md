# Variational Autoencoder Learning Roadmap

This directory follows the problem-based VAE route in the companion reading
guide.  The scripts are algorithm lessons, not a production training
framework: each lesson keeps its objective, probability interfaces, gradient
boundaries, and sampling order visible.  Repeated network blocks, dataset
plumbing, and evaluation bookkeeping live in `dl_utils/vae/`.

`0.1_autoencoder.py` and `1.0_vae_train.py` are frozen introductory
baselines.  They retain their original 256x256 face setup.  All later lessons
use 32x32 or 64x64 images, small single-GPU models, and only the persistence
needed by a real stage boundary or a separate evaluation script.

The group numbers encode the recommended reading order.  They do not claim
that beta-VAE, hierarchical VAE, and VQ-VAE form one inheritance chain.

## 1. Completed Baseline

| Lesson | Role |
|---|---|
| `0.1_autoencoder.py` | Deterministic reconstruction baseline |
| `1.0_vae_train.py` | Standard Gaussian VAE and reparameterization |
| `1.1_vae_analysis.py` | Posterior reconstruction versus prior generation |

The first two files are intentionally excluded from this refactor.  Later
lessons reuse their probability concepts, not their large face architecture.

## 2. Conditional Probability Interface

| Lesson | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `2.0_conditional_vae.py` | Add condition-aware prior, posterior, and decoder interfaces | conditional distortion/rate and per-class prior samples |
| `2.1_conditional_vae_evaluation.py` | Separate generation-time and training-time information paths | condition compliance, within-condition diversity, prior ablation, and shuffled-condition checks |

The target image is visible to the recognition posterior during training but
never to the conditional prior used for generation.

## 3. Inference Objective and Posterior Expressiveness

| Lesson | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `3.0_iwae.py` | Replace the one-sample ELBO with a stable per-example K-particle bound | K=1 identity, validation bound, ESS/K, and gradient statistics |
| `3.1_iaf_vae.py` | Transform the variational posterior with a short inverse autoregressive flow | exact log-determinant, scale range, and Gaussian-versus-flow comparison |
| `3.2_inference_evaluation.py` | Compare bound and posterior-family axes under one protocol | held-out large-K estimate, rate, active units, reconstruction, and samples |

IWAE changes the training bound.  IAF changes `q(z | x)`.  Neither changes
the prior generation path into a flow-based generator.

## 4. Information Rate and Disentanglement Boundary

| Lesson | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `4.0_beta_vae.py` | Multiply the complete sample KL by beta | rate-distortion and capacity/active-unit profiles |
| `4.1_beta_tc_vae.py` | Add a minibatch-weighted aggregated-posterior TC term | decomposition logs and matched-rate comparison |
| `4.2_disentanglement_evaluation.py` | Evaluate on procedurally controlled factors and multiple seeds | factor prediction, latent-factor alignment, rate, and reconstruction |

FactorVAE remains a historical comparison in the reading guide.  It estimates
the same total-correlation object with a discriminator, so this repository
does not retain a second two-optimizer implementation.  No single traversal
or TC estimate is treated as proof of semantic or causal disentanglement.

## 5. Hierarchical Gaussian Latents

| Lesson | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `5.0_hierarchical_vae.py` | Make `p(z2) p(z1 | z2) p(x | z1)` and both KL pairings explicit | per-layer rate, active units, and top-down sampling |
| `5.1_ladder_vae.py` | Fuse bottom-up evidence with the matching top-down prior by precision | the same metrics under a directly comparable generator |
| `5.2_hierarchical_vae_evaluation.py` | Intervene on one stochastic layer at a time | posterior/prior replacement and layer-closing counterfactuals |

Warm-up and group-wise free bits change collapse incentives; they do not prove
that either layer carries useful information.  NVAE and VDVAE are retained as
structure-level scale-up endpoints, and VDM only as a bridge to the diffusion
series; none receives a misleading small "paper reproduction" here.

## 6. Discrete Visual Tokenizers

| Lesson | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `6.0_vq_vae.py` | Nearest-neighbor quantization, STE, codebook and commitment losses | reconstruction, entropy, utilization, and frozen PixelCNN prior |
| `6.1_vq_vae_2.py` | Add top/bottom token grids and `p(top) p(bottom | top)` | top-first sampling and hierarchy interventions |
| `6.2_fsq.py` | Replace learned vector lookup with fixed scalar levels | matched encoder/decoder and prior budget |
| `6.3_discrete_tokenizer_evaluation.py` | Compare tokenizer and downstream-prior behavior | distortion, effective rate, reconstruction distribution, and generation |

Tokenizer training and prior training are separate stages.  Nominal
vocabulary size, active codes, empirical entropy, and prior difficulty are
reported separately.  dVAE is a historical stochastic-categorical coordinate,
not another implementation.

## 7. Perceptual Discrete Compression

| Lesson | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `7.0_vae_gan.py` | Learn reconstruction similarity in discriminator features | three optimizer boundaries and paired fidelity |
| `7.1_vqgan.py` | Add frozen feature distance, PatchGAN, adaptive adversarial weight, and a Transformer token prior | tokenizer/prior stage separation and sharpness-versus-fidelity diagnostics |

VAE-GAN is a focused historical bridge rather than a full reproduction.
VQGAN retains the complete discrete tokenizer-to-prior system.  A frozen VGG
feature distance is named as such and is never mislabeled as LPIPS.

## 8. Continuous Latent Compression

| Lesson | Visible increment | Focused evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `8.0_kl_autoencoder.py` | Replace VQ with a weakly regularized spatial Gaussian posterior | sample/mean reconstruction, latent scale, and frozen encode/decode contract |
| `8.1_perceptual_autoencoder_evaluation.py` | Compare KL-AE and VQGAN under one held-out protocol | paired distortion, feature distance, reconstruction distribution, and latent statistics |

A KL autoencoder is a compressor, not an unconditional generator.  Standard
normal decodes are interface diagnostics only; a trained diffusion or flow
model must learn the aggregate continuous latent distribution.

## 9. Resource, Checkpoint, and Evaluation Contract

- Simple 32x32 Gaussian lessons train directly and save one final artifact only
  when a later evaluation consumes it.  They do not implement resume logic.
- Discrete and perceptual models save stage-boundary artifacts because the
  tokenizer must be frozen before its prior or downstream model is trained.
  A SHA-256 state identity binds each prior to the exact frozen tokenizer.
- Smoke tests use synthetic tensors, perform the relevant backward passes, and
  never download data or pretrained weights.
- A separate evaluation script exists only when it adds a model-specific
  comparison or intervention.  Training diagnostics are not duplicated under
  a grander name.
- Reconstruction, prior generation, and downstream latent generation remain
  distinct paths.  Results always identify which path produced an image.
- The KL-AE checkpoint binds its exact state identity, dataset-level scale,
  mean/sample encoding choice, and inverse-scale decode rule to downstream
  latent models.
- Advanced modern tokenizers (MAGVIT-v2, TiTok, DC-AE, RAE, AToken, WeTok,
  SVG, and RepTok) are revisited after LDM and DiT, when their compression and
  denoising trade-offs can be evaluated in context.
